## 5. What it means

- 79 of 112 datapoints are refused by reader defects in four shapes; 15 are correct refusals (10 of them leads for the wrong wine), 14 wait on a model gap (one wine where the estate makes two colours) and 4 on reading a web page's structure.
- Clearing a defect does not by itself make a datapoint accepted: each still goes through the fiche reader's figures and the statement check, which the P1 review never reached for these claims.
- A reader change is its own phase: tools/sheetreader.py and tools/read-fiches.py are fingerprinted into every evidence decision, so any change is a new decider version, re-run against every held decision.
