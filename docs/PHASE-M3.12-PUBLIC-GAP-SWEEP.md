# Phase M3.12 — Bordeaux-wide public missing-data sweep

Generated by `python tools/m312-public-gap-sweep.py` (`data/audit/public-gap-sweep.jsonl`); never edited by hand. **An audit:** nothing in the model, the evidence store, the discovery store, the source profiles or the app changed. Every candidate is a lead (`review_status` NOT_REVIEWED, `reader_status` NOT_RUN): discovery, not review, acceptance or application.

## 1. Scope and method

- **Surface:** 5292 vintages of 384 wines (every vintage the model holds) × the 7 fields the value runner applies (alc, blend, months, oak, ph, pick, yld) = 37044 datapoints; 12232 hold a canonical value, **24812 are missing**.
- **Missing vintages:** only where a held public document of one estate is headed with one year none of the estate's wines holds (99 leads). An absent year alone is never one.
- **Public web:** the discovery crawl of 2026-10-25 (robots respected; 29517 documents held from the estates' own sites), the M3.3-M3.11 audits and the model's documents and evidence. Nothing was fetched: a new crawl writes `data/discovery`, which the model is built from, so it is its own phase (§8).
- **Tiers:** A, the estate's own domain (or its M3.7-confirmed estate-controlled one); A-shared, a group domain serving several estates (first-party for the group; which estate a document is stays to confirm - the M3.5 Bernaleau lesson), never P1; B, none (the rules admit no external source for these fields); C, any other host.
- **A candidate document** must be held, extracted, not a site's home page, mapped by the crawl's record to one estate and one year, and carry at least one vintage-field label in its harvested text. Spot-checked: a Dubourdieu group-site sheet mapped to Doisy-Daëne was Château Haura's, and Lamont group sheets offered as Château d'Argan vintages were other wines - why A-shared leads stay below P1 and missing-vintage leads need the document's wine.

## 8. Where public evidence appears unavailable, and proposals

- **NO_PUBLIC_EVIDENCE** (10670): the estate's reached site holds no document of the year, the held documents do not state the field, no first-party domain is known, or the vintage was not produced.
- **BLOCKED_PUBLIC_ROUTE** (529): robots.txt disallows the route, or the site refuses crawling; respected.
- **Proposal A, a targeted crawl** of the 13 first-party domains not fully reached (UNREACHABLE, PARTIAL, no crawl record) with the existing crawler, in its own phase: it writes `data/discovery`. 959 datapoints wait on it.
- **Proposal B, a retry** of the 90 first-party PDF URLs whose fetch failed, by the same crawler.
- **Proposal C, field readers** for months, oak and pick: the held text is checked for alc, blend, ph and yld labels only (`data_gap_analysis.LABEL_FIELD`), so 11127 datapoints on held documents stay UNKNOWN. Extending the harvest labels is a reader-side change and its own phase.
- **Proposal D, the OCR backlog** (EXTRACTION_FAILED, 99 datapoints).
