## 1. Baseline

- Measured on the model as committed at M3.3 (`357f44c`): 1,403 vintages hold no document, at 126 estates.
- M3.3 found that for some of them the evidence store has ACCEPTED a held document for exactly that wine and vintage, yet the vintage-documents index does not link it: the legacy attribution named the estate only.
- An accepted decision is not taken as proof of the wine. Its target wine came from the legacy manifest, and `is_second=False` only says no second-wine name was found. The document itself must establish the mapping.
- Rules, applied in order; the first that fails decides:
  1. the wine is not governed by an open identity decision;
  2. the document is not already linked, and its bytes are held;
  3. the accepted decisions on the document name one estate, one wine and one vintage;
  4. the document has readable text (stored text, or the held PDF read as `tools/sheetreader.py` reads it);
  5. its opening (first 600 characters) states the vintage, not several, and not only as a calendar date; its body does not head three or more other vintages;
  6. it names no other wine of the estate, and does not speak of "the other wine" or a named cuvée;
  7. it names the wine: by its distinctive name, or by its full name within 60 characters of the year, or says "grand vin" in its opening (not "grand vin de Bordeaux", not "grand vin sec");
  8. its stated blend is of the wine's colour, and it does not describe the estate's red and white alike;
  9. a v5 evidence link ties the accepted decision to the model ids.
- Never used as evidence of identity: the filename, the URL path, the PDF metadata, the estate alone.

## 8. Patterns

- **Group sheets carry several estates.** 134 of the 142 conflicts are documents of one producer group whose sheets were accepted for two of its estates at once (the decisions name Château Mongravey and Château de Braude). The sheet names one château; the other estate's acceptance is the error, and is for evidence review, not for this audit.
- **Estate-and-vintage sheets.** 152 candidates name the estate and the year, never the wine. Most are a producer's series whose title is an image, or vintage pages that describe the season, not a wine. They stay unmapped: the grand vin is not assumed.
- **Vintage compilations.** 95 candidates are documents that carry several vintages (a press digest, a vertical, a multi-year sheet), or whose opening states a different year. The accepted decision chose one year among several.
- **One model wine, several real ones.** Where the model lists one wine and the estate makes more, a sheet for the unlisted wine looks like the listed one. The colour test caught white sheets filed under a red wine and a document describing the red and white alike (28); the sibling test caught a second wine whose name lies inside the estate's.
- **Most refusals concentrate in few estates:** Château Doisy-Daëne 79, Château de Braude 72, Château Mongravey 65, Château Desmirail 36, Château Latour 31, Clos Floridène 26.
