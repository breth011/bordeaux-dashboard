## 4. Evidence and provenance preservation

- **What a mapping is.** Each mapping is one new Attribution (method `DOCUMENT_TEXT`, status `ACCEPTED`, role `FIRST`) beside the artifact's manifest attribution. The manifest attribution named the estate only, and is not changed.
- **Its provenance.** Each Attribution has its own DOCUMENT provenance (actor `tools/m34b-apply.py`, stream `M3.4B`). It quotes the audit's basis, records the sha256 and the accepted decision id(s) (the evidence-link row key), and derives from the artifact's original `LEGACY_MANIFEST` provenance.
- **Nothing rewritten.** No evidence decision, artifact, content hash, placement, URL or earlier attribution was changed or removed.
- **Decisions kept beside the mapping.** M3.4A listed every v5 link on the document, so 2 of its references are not acceptances for this target:
  - Château de Braude 2019 `ar_002515`: REFUSED WRONG_VINTAGE for 1984, which agrees with the mapping. The guard kept the 2019 sheet off 1984.
  - Château de Braude 2019 `ar_004159`: REFUSED WRONG_VINTAGE for 1984, which agrees with the mapping. The guard kept the 2019 sheet off 1984.
  - The manifest keeps them as `other_decisions`. The mapping rests only on the accepted ones.
- Noted in preflight: open CONFLICT finding qf_6bf494e8f677 on the estate is not touched: the mapping is the grand vin's, and the document names no other wine.
- **What a mapping does not mean:** that the document's figures are accepted, that it is the source of any field, or anything about other vintages, the wine's history or its identity.
