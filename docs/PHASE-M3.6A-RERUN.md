## 3. How each document was judged

- **A web page** is judged by `tools/web_evidence.py` on its stored structure:
  - Its identity is its visible main heading. The tab title, URL, filename, folder and domain are never read.
  - Only its content region counts. Navigation, sidebars, related products, recommendation, archive and list regions are excluded, and their matches are kept only as diagnostics.
  - Its page type is decided before any mapping, and its vintage must come from the heading or a labelled field.
- **A PDF** is judged by the M3.5 rubric. A PDF with no title is judged by M3.4A's rules, unchanged.
- **The v5 reader** (`whose_sheet`, unmodified) is a veto, never a basis. A SAFE_TO_MAP needs the document's own evidence and no objection from the reader. A reader acceptance the evidence rejects is recorded as a reader false positive, with its class.
- **The R1 gate.** Every SAFE_TO_MAP is checked again: a wine document, its main heading the wine's exact name, its vintage from the heading or a labelled field, nothing from an excluded region, no date-only year.
