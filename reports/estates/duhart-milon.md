## Findings
**Two pairs of duplicate tasting notes**, on Moulin de Duhart at
2005/2006 and 2007/2008, plus the grand vin at 2016/2017 — in every case
the later vintage carrying the earlier's passage. That is the pattern
Lafite's sheets showed outright, at an estate that shares Lafite's
ownership and its sheet design. They are not pinned, because unlike
Lafite 1976 none of them names its year inside itself, and which of the
pair is the original cannot be settled from the documents alone.
