## Findings
**Thirteen season notes ran into the analysis block** — the most of any
estate. Its sheets print the phenology and the figures as one flattened
run: *"…with a very good constitution. **Alcohol: 12,95 Total acidity:
3,5** Weighing of the crop…"*. A label, a colon and a digit now ends a
passage; that whole shape is what makes it safe without a
sentence-boundary test, since a note may use the word alcohol but does
not write "Alcohol: 13,5".

**Sarget de Gruaud-Larose heads its notes with three headings run
together** — "CLIMAT MILLESIME ASSEMBLAGE" — and the capitals-stripping
loop stopped on the first word, because the bare `CLIMAT` was not in its
list. Added, with `ASSEMBLAGE`.

**14 of its 32 missing addresses recovered** by reading the links off its
own pages.
