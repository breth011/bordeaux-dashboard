## Findings
**Its 1995 tasting note is its 1994's**, word for word, and the two come
from different pages of the estate's own site — so the site repeats it,
as Lafite's sheets do at 1975/1976 and 2007/2016. The later vintage is
pinned absent.

**Eighteen decanting instructions were inside the tasting notes.** The
estate prints *"For maximum enjoyment Decant the wine 2 hours before
serving."* under every vintage, and not one record had its `decant` field
filled. The decant label is `decantation|carafage|decanting` — a heading
with text under it — and Malescot's is a sentence with the verb in it and
no heading, so nothing matched. Both halves of the phrase are required,
at the very end of the field.
