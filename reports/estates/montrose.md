## Findings
Estate 43 of 61, brought forward because its second wine had documents
waiting — and because they were filed under another estate's wine.

**`BDX_CUVEE_WINES` listed "La Vieille Dame de Trottevieille" under
Montrose.** That is Trotte Vieille's second wine, and Trotte Vieille
lists it too. Nineteen of Montrose's documents were filed under it, and
nine records existed on the key
`montrose|la-vieille-dame-de-trottevieille`.

Every one of them is La Dame de Montrose:

```
Dame-de-Montrose-2017-FT-FR.pdf
Fiche_technique_LA_DAME_DE_MONTROSE_EST_RO_2019_Francais.pdf
Fiche-Technique-Dame-de-Montrose-2022-GB.pdf
DA-2023-EN.pdf          — opens "DAME DE MONTROSE 2023"
```

and their blends run to 76% Merlot against 24% Cabernet Sauvignon, which
is a second wine's shape and Montrose's own: the grand vin runs the
other way round.

The nine records are re-keyed to `la-dame-de-montrose`, the eighteen
rows re-pointed, and the Trottevieille entry taken off Montrose's list.
It stays on Trotte Vieille's.

**The nineteenth was the grand vin's own sheet.**
`Fiche_technique_Chateau_MONTROSE_EST_RO_2021_Francais.pdf` opens "2021
Un millésime de Grand Terroir" and names Montrose three times. It was
filed as the second wine's and is back to `grand`.
