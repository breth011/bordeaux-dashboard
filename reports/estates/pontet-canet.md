## Findings
**Eight records were flagged for a blend equal to the planting, and all
eight were right.** `plantings.js` held CS 65, ME 30, CF 4, PV 1 for
Pontet-Canet, read from its 2003 sheet under the label "grape varieties",
and the app drew it behind every vintage's blend as what the vineyard is
planted with.

That label's figures change on every sheet:

```
2009   Grape varieties: 65 % Cabernet Sauvignon - 30 % Merlot
                        4 % Cabernet Franc - 1 % Petit Verdot
2011   Grape varieties: 60 % Cabernet Sauvignon - 35 % Merlot
                        4 % Cabernet Franc - 1 % Petit Verdot
2016   Grape varieties: 65 % Cabernet Sauvignon - 30 % Merlot
                        3 % Cabernet Franc - 2 % Petit Verdot
```

A figure that moves every year is not a planting. At this estate "grape
varieties" means the assemblage, so the entry was the **2003 blend
wearing the vineyard's name** — and the eight records agreeing with it
were correct all along. The planting entry is removed; the blends stay,
and they vary properly across 26 vintages, CS 52–70 and ME 22–43.
